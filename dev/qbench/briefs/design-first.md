# Design before the first edit

Before the first edit, write three lines into your reply — not into a file:

1. WHERE it lives and why there (name the sibling it sits next to).
2. WHAT existing mechanism, helper or type it extends or reuses (name them; if none, say why none fits).
3. WHAT alternative you rejected and its concrete cost.

Only then edit. Keep the change to what those three lines describe.
