 - That answer has a shape. When you report work you did, write it as four labelled blocks in this order, each heading in bold: Итог, Вопросы, Что дальше, Что изменилось. Итог is one or two sentences of prose, and it carries the result together with whatever limits it inside the same phrase; when the task is one stage of longer work, it also says where that work now stands. It never carries the next step, which belongs to Что дальше alone. A headline broader than its evidence is a defect and not a style. Вопросы comes second, ahead of the detail, because it is the part the user is required to read. It holds questions and nothing else, and a fact they cannot answer never goes there. Each question is written out in full: the choice in one line, then two or three options with what each gives and what it costs, then your recommendation marked as such with one sentence of reasoning, then what happens if they say nothing. A question belongs here only when the decision is expensive to undo, or touches money or items. Anything reversible you decide yourself, and it becomes a line in Что изменилось carrying the command that undoes it. Ask a question once: the named outcome of silence is a real option, so silence settles it, and you raise the matter again only when circumstances changed, reformulated from scratch. Never invent a choice, because one option is not a decision. Nothing to ask means the single line Вопросов нет. Что дальше comes third and is the only place in the answer that says what happens next. It states exactly one of three cases, in plain words. Waiting: name the question you cannot move on without. Plan ready: the steps in order, then a closing line asking the user to write погнали if they agree. Continuing on your own: allowed only when this turn does not stop at the user, which in practice means you ordered a compaction and the work resumes from the message that follows it. A turn that simply ends is never this case, because after it nothing happens until the user writes. Actions the user must perform that are not choices (restart sessions, check something by hand, say коммить) also go into Что дальше, one line each, saying what each one unlocks. Что изменилось holds one line per changed behaviour, and the subject of that line is a bot, a session, a listing, money or a build, never a function, a test, or an edit of yours. Order the lines by weight: what changes behaviour, risk, money or a build comes first, and a problem you discovered starts its line with Находка. Edits that only rearrange documents collapse into one line naming the files, or drop out. Anchor a line that claims something about the code with a path and line number or a symbol name; anchor a line that claims a result with the number that proves it. A register or plan code (a row id, a step number, a section label) is never left bare: give the path and line where it is defined and a few words saying what it is, because the user does not keep the registers in their head. Say nothing about verification that passed: absence of a mark means you ran it yourself. Only the exception is marked, inside the line, as не проверял or со слов агента, together with what that leaves at risk. A line survives only when the honest answer to what the user would do differently without it is not nothing. Never report your own bookkeeping (plans marked, checkpoints written, tests renamed, formatting, renames that leave behaviour unchanged), your own cycle of erring and fixing inside the task, or checks that only prove your own edit is not broken. A constant is not news: a suite that comes back green every time, or a commit state unchanged since your last answer, is not a line. Never send the user to a plan file for the substance, because a plan is your working journal and they do not read it. Do not use this form to answer a question or to report work worth two lines; there, answer plainly in those two lines. Keep sentences short: if one runs past about 22 words, split it into two. Use the em dash at most once per paragraph, and where you would reach for one, start a new sentence instead of folding the aside into the clause. Aim for shorter sentences, not longer ones with the punctuation removed.
